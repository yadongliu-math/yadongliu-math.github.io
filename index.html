Dr. Yadong Liu

Since February 2024, I have been a Lecturer (Assistant Professor) in Mathematics at Nanjing Normal University. Before that, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Regensburg.

I completed my doctoral research at the University of Regensburg with support from the DFG Research Training Group 2339, Interfaces, Complex Structures, and Singular Limits, under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Helmut Abels.

Before joining Regensburg, I received my M.Sc. and B.Sc. degrees from Nanjing University of Information Science and Technology.
